YouTube thumbnail entry point

Read ../SEO/YouTube_Packaging_Common.md first. It owns action modes, source fidelity, visual preferences, limits, export profiles, and testing rules.

For long videos, read and follow ./SEO_Thumbnail_Long.md.
For YouTube Shorts, read and follow ./SEO_Thumbnail_Short.md.
Resolve the intended asset from the actual source and request; do not rely on the folder name alone.

Honor the requested action: discussion produces discussion, concepts produce concepts, and explicit generation produces images. Editing these prompts does not execute them.

Use the shared defaults unless the user overrides them. Keep title-copy coordination through ../SEO/SEO_yt_Long.md or ../SEO/SEO_yt_Short.md, then serialize approved metadata with ../SEO/SEO_yt_JSON.md when requested.

Do not maintain another independent set of thumbnail rules in this entry point.
