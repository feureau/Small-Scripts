ESSAY RULES

LENGTH AND SHAPE
- Length: 400 to 800 words. Count words before finalizing.
- The essay is prose. It is not a bullet-point document with prose connectors.
- Between 3 and 5 ALL CAPS subheadings divide the essay into sections.
- Each section under a subheading must be a minimum of 3 sentences and should normally run as a single developed paragraph or two connected paragraphs. A subheading followed by one sentence, or by a bulleted list with no surrounding prose, is a failure and must be rebuilt.
- Bullet points are permitted only inside one or two sections, only when the content is genuinely enumerable (a list of cognates, a list of deliverables), and only when each bullet is followed or preceded by at least two sentences of prose that give it context and consequence. Never let a section consist of a heading plus bullets alone.
- Short declarative fragments are not a style. Every sentence must be a complete thought that could be read aloud as part of a flowing paragraph.

STRUCTURE
- The essay must follow this overall arc, in this order:
  1. Orientation (2 to 3 sentences, no subheading above it): What this is and who it is for. The first 2 to 3 sentences must contain the primary keyword and the core hook. The first sentence must answer "Why should I stay?"
  2. The deliverable: What the supporter actually gets. Be specific about format, cadence, and what is included. Write it as prose, not as a list.
  3. The evidence or narrative body: 2 to 3 sections under ALL CAPS subheadings that carry the actual substance. This is where the long-form weight of the essay lives.
  4. The outcome framing: Why this matters, what support enables. Framed as an outcome, not a request.
  5. One section may contain a bulleted list of deliverables if enumerable, wrapped in prose before and after.
  6. The call-to-action and closing question.

SUBHEADING RULES
- Subheadings should tell a story, not label a section. "WHAT WILKINSON ACTUALLY WROTE" is a story beat. "Evidence" is a label and is forbidden.
- Subheadings are in ALL CAPS, no punctuation at the end.
- Every subheading must be followed by at least three sentences of prose before any list appears.

KEYWORD RULES

CLOSING

CUT RULE
